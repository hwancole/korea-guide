Christmas is a big, festive season in Seoul even though fewer than a third of Koreans are Christian. Department stores compete with giant LED façades, the city runs a free winter festival around Gwanghwamun, and Jamsil hosts a European-style Christmas market. It feels closer to a romantic city-lights holiday than a family one: in Korea, Christmas Eve is mainly a date night for couples.

> **Quick answer:** For 2026, the main free events are the **Seoul Winter Festival** (11 December 2026 to 31 January 2027) around **Gwanghwamun Square**, Cheonggyecheon and DDP, with the **Gwanghwamun Market** Christmas market, light shows and the **Seoul Plaza ice rink**. The **Lotte Town Christmas Market** in Jamsil runs **19 November 2026 to 3 January 2027** and uses timed advance tickets. Shinsegae's famous **Myeongdong media façade** plays daily **5 PM to midnight** until 31 January 2027. **25 December is a public holiday** in Korea, but most shops, restaurants and attractions stay open.

All dates below are as of November 2026. Hours for the city festival programmes had not been published yet, so where only last season's details exist, they are marked as such.

## Seoul Winter Festival: Gwanghwamun, Cheonggyecheon and DDP

Seoul's city-run winter festival (서울윈터페스타, recently also listed as 서울윈터페스티벌) ties together the capital's best free winter events. The official Seoul festival portal lists it from **11 December 2026 to 31 January 2027** at Gwanghwamun Square and six other sites.

### Gwanghwamun Market

This is the closest thing Seoul has to a classic European Christmas market. Expect wooden booths selling ornaments, handmade gifts and winter snacks from small Korean businesses, plus photo zones and free hands-on activities. Last season it had over 100 sellers, entry was free and it opened **in the evenings only** (reported as roughly 5:30–9:30 PM, staying open until midnight on 31 December). The 2026 market is reported to run from 11 December to 10 January, which means it should be open over Christmas and New Year's Eve this time.

Getting there: Gwanghwamun Station (Line 5) connects directly to the square. City Hall Station (Lines 1 and 2) and Gyeongbokgung Station (Line 3) are about a 10-minute walk.

### Seoul Light Gwanghwamun

After dark, the buildings and gate around the square become a giant screen for projection-mapping shows, with light sculptures on the square itself. It shares the market's area, so you can see both in one evening. It also hosts a New Year countdown (see our [New Year's Eve in Seoul guide](../new-years-eve-seoul-bosingak-bell/)).

### Seoul Lantern Festival on Cheonggyecheon

The lantern festival (서울빛초롱축제) fills the Cheonggyecheon stream with hundreds of illuminated sculptures and media artworks, running roughly from Cheonggye Plaza east toward Samil Bridge. It starts a short walk from Gwanghwamun Square, so a good route is: market, then light show, then a walk down the stream. The path along the water is narrow and busy on weekend evenings, so weekdays are calmer.

### Seoul Light DDP

Dongdaemun Design Plaza's curved façade becomes a huge media-art screen, with a "Christmas town" area outside. It is a good add-on if you are shopping in Dongdaemun at night.

### Seoul Plaza ice rink

The outdoor rink in front of City Hall is one of Seoul's best-value winter activities. It is reported to open from 18 December 2026 to 9 February 2027. Last season, a one-hour session cost **₩1,000 including skates and a helmet**, hours were about 10 AM–9:30 PM (until 11 PM on Saturdays and holidays), and sessions could be booked online at seoulskate.kr. The rink closes temporarily for heavy snow, rain or high fine-dust levels. Use City Hall Station, exit 5 or 6.

## Lotte Town Christmas Market, Jamsil

The most "Christmas-card" market in Seoul is at the foot of Lotte World Tower, Korea's tallest building. For 2026 it runs **19 November 2026 to 3 January 2027** on the outdoor World Park lawn, with a 19th-century British theme: red-brick-style stalls, a 17-metre Victorian clock tower, a carousel, a big Christmas tree, carol music, British-style food such as fish and chips, and stalls selling Christmas goods. A media show plays on the Lotte World Mall façade in the evenings.

**Tickets:** entry works on timed slots booked in advance, released in rounds. For 2026, the November slots opened on 9 November, December slots on 20 November, and year-end slots on 7 December. Last season, daytime entry before 4 PM was free and evening entry cost ₩5,000 through Naver booking, with last entry at 9:30 PM. Evening slots on Christmas weekends sell out fast, so book as soon as your round opens.

**Getting there:** Jamsil Station (Lines 2 and 8). The market is a few minutes' walk through or around Lotte World Mall. You can combine it with Seokchon Lake and the Seoul Sky observatory.

## Myeongdong: department store light shows and the cathedral

Myeongdong is the easiest place to see Seoul's Christmas lights without planning anything, and it is all free.

- **Shinsegae Department Store main store:** its whole façade is a giant LED screen. The 2026 Christmas film premiered on 6 November and plays **every day from 5 PM to midnight until 31 January 2027**. It is a 3-minute-40-second 3D-style animation set to a carol arrangement of Beethoven's Ninth. The best view is from across the roundabout in front of the store. Use Hoehyeon Station (Line 4), exit 7.
- **Lotte Department Store main store:** a short walk from Shinsegae, Lotte decorates its building and has announced a new large media façade for the 2026 holiday season. Use Euljiro 1-ga Station (Line 2).
- **Myeongdong Cathedral:** Korea's best-known Catholic cathedral puts up lit trees and a nativity scene. It is an active church, so keep your voice down and avoid blocking the steps during services. It is about a five-minute uphill walk from Myeongdong Station (Line 4).

Myeongdong gets extremely crowded on the evenings of 24 and 25 December. If you only want photos of the façades, a weekday evening in early December is much easier.

## Is Seoul open on Christmas Day?

**Yes, mostly.** Christmas Day (성탄절) is a national public holiday in Korea. In 2026 it falls on a **Friday**, so it is a long weekend for locals. What that means for you:

- **Open:** department stores, malls, Olive Young and most shops, cafés, most restaurants, the subway and buses, and major attractions. Palaces are generally open as usual unless the 25th is their weekly closing day.
- **Closed:** banks, post offices and government offices. Some small family-run restaurants take the day off.
- **Busy:** Christmas Eve (a Thursday in 2026) is the big night out. Popular restaurants, especially Western-style ones, are often fully booked weeks ahead for 24 and 25 December, and many offer set Christmas menus at higher prices. Book early if you want a special dinner.

Christmas in Korea is not a gift-heavy family holiday as in much of the West. Couples go on dates, friends meet up, and many people buy a Christmas cake.

## Tips for visiting Seoul at Christmas

1. **Dress for the cold.** December evenings in Seoul are often below freezing, and you will be outdoors at the markets and light shows. See our [Korean winter weather guide](../korea-winter-weather-what-to-wear/).
2. **Go on a weekday.** The difference between a Tuesday and a Saturday evening at Gwanghwamun or Jamsil is huge.
3. **Plan an evening loop.** Gwanghwamun Market, Cheonggyecheon lanterns and the Myeongdong façades are all within about 2 km of each other in central Seoul.
4. **Use the subway.** Roads around the festival sites are congested in the evening, and the subway is quicker. Our [Seoul subway guide](../seoul-subway-guide/) explains T-money and routes.
5. **Carry a card.** Market stalls usually take cards; a WOWPASS or travel card works well. See [paying in Korea](../paying-in-korea-cards-cash-wowpass/).

## FAQ

**Does Seoul have Christmas markets?**
Yes. The two main ones are the free Gwanghwamun Market (part of the Seoul Winter Festival, evenings only) and the ticketed Lotte Town Christmas Market in Jamsil. Department stores also run smaller indoor holiday markets.

**When does Seoul start decorating for Christmas?**
Department stores start in early November (Shinsegae's 2026 façade started on 6 November). The Lotte Town market opens in mid to late November, and the city's winter festival starts in mid-December.

**Is the Gwanghwamun Christmas market free?**
Entry has been free every year, and you only pay for what you buy at the booths.

**Do I need to book the Lotte World Christmas market?**
Yes, entry uses timed slots booked online in advance, released in rounds. Book as soon as the round for your dates opens, especially for evenings and weekends.

**Is it worth visiting Seoul at Christmas?**
If you like city lights, markets and winter food, yes. Hotel prices rise around Christmas and New Year, and the coldest weather comes in January, so mid-December is a good balance.

**Is there snow in Seoul at Christmas?**
Sometimes, but a white Christmas is not guaranteed. Light snow is more common in January. For a sure snowy scene, consider a day at a ski resort; see our [skiing in Korea guide](../skiing-in-korea-from-seoul/).
