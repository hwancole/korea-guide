Nami Island (남이섬, *Namiseom*) is a small, tree-lined island in the Bukhan River, about 60 km northeast of Seoul. It is famous for its rows of metasequoia and ginkgo trees, and it is one of the easiest day trips you can do from the city without a tour.

> **Quick answer:** Take the **ITX-Cheongchun** train from Yongsan or Cheongnyangni (or the cheaper **Gyeongchun Line** subway from Sangbong) to **Gapyeong Station**, then a 5-minute taxi or 20-minute walk to **Gapyeong Wharf**. The ferry to the island takes about **5 minutes** and is included in the admission ticket: **19,000 won for adults**, as of November 2026. Ferries run from about **8 a.m. to 9 p.m.** every 10 to 20 minutes in the daytime. Allow about 1 to 1.5 hours each way from central Seoul and 2 to 4 hours on the island.

Prices and hours below are as of November 2026, from the island's official site unless stated otherwise. They change by season, so check again close to your trip.

## How to get from Seoul to Nami Island

There are three practical ways to do it on your own. All the rail options end at Gapyeong Station, about 1.5 km from the ferry wharf.

| Option | From | Time to Gapyeong | Cost (one way) | Good for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ITX-Cheongchun train | Yongsan | About 60 min | 6,900 won | Comfort, reserved seat, central start |
| ITX-Cheongchun train | Cheongnyangni | About 42-45 min | 5,700 won | The fastest rail option |
| Gyeongchun Line (subway) | Sangbong (Line 7) | About 50 min from Sangbong | Normal subway fare, tap your transport card | No booking, cheapest |
| Official shuttle bus | Hongik Univ., Myeongdong, DDP | About 1.5 h | Check with the operator | No transfers, set times |

ITX fares are from a guide published in October 2026; Korail can adjust them, and the app shows the exact fare when you book.

### Option 1: ITX-Cheongchun (the easiest)

ITX-Cheongchun is a double-decker express train that runs between Seoul and Chuncheon and stops at Gapyeong. Seats are reserved, so book a seat rather than turning up and hoping.

1. **Book in the KorailTalk app** or on the Korail website. Search for Yongsan (용산) or Cheongnyangni (청량리) to Gapyeong (가평). Weekend mornings in spring and autumn sell out, so book a few days ahead.
2. **Board at Yongsan or Cheongnyangni.** Check the departure board for the platform, then stand by your car number.
3. **Get off at Gapyeong.** It is the stop before Gangchon and Chuncheon, so keep an eye on the screen.

Trains run from early morning until about 10 p.m. Book your return at the same time, because the last trains back to Seoul on autumn weekends fill up.

### Option 2: Gyeongchun Line subway (the cheapest)

The Gyeongchun Line is part of the Seoul Metropolitan Subway network, so you pay with a T-money or WOWPASS card like on any other line (see our [Seoul subway guide](../seoul-subway-guide/)).

1. Take Line 7 (or the Gyeongui-Jungang Line) to **Sangbong** (상봉) and transfer to the Gyeongchun Line toward **Chuncheon** (춘천). Some Gyeongchun trains start further west at Cheongnyangni or Gwangundae.
2. Ride to **Gapyeong** (가평), about 50 minutes from Sangbong.
3. Tap out with the same card. The fare is the regular subway fare plus a distance charge, deducted automatically.

The trade-off is time: with the transfer, plan on 1.5 to 2 hours from central Seoul, and trains are less frequent than city lines.

### Option 3: The official shuttle bus

Nami Island's website lists a daily round-trip shuttle run by **Ktourstory**:

| Direction | Departures |
|---|---|
| Seoul to Nami Island | Hongik University Station 8:20, Namsan Yejang bus parking lot (near Myeongdong Station) 9:00, DDP 9:10, arriving about 10:40 |
| Nami Island to Seoul | Leaves 16:30, arriving DDP about 18:00, Myeongdong 18:10, Hongik University 18:50 |

It is sold as a round trip only and must be booked in advance on the Ktourstory website. It is the simplest option if you do not want to deal with trains, but you are tied to the fixed return time.

### From Gapyeong Station to the wharf

Nami Island's official directions put Gapyeong Wharf **1.5 km** from Gapyeong Station:

- **Taxi:** about 5 minutes, from the rank outside the station. A short base-fare-range ride; see our [taxi guide](../taxi-in-korea-kakao-t-guide/) for how to pay.
- **Bus 10-4:** about 5 minutes.
- **Walk:** about 20 minutes, mostly flat.

For the map app, search **"Nami Island Gapyeong Wharf"** or the Korean **남이섬 선착장**. The address is 1024 Bukhangangbyeon-ro, Gapyeong-eup, Gapyeong-gun. Naver Map and Kakao Map work better than Google here; see our [map apps guide](../google-maps-korea-naver-kakao-map/).

## Nami Island tickets in 2026

Nami Island styles itself as the "Naminara Republic", so the admission ticket is called a "visa". The ticket office is at Gapyeong Wharf, and the price includes the round-trip ferry.

| Ticket | Round-trip ferry | Motor boat in, ferry out |
|---|---|---|
| Adult | 19,000 won | 29,000 won |
| Middle and high school students, seniors 70+ | 16,000 won | 26,000 won |
| Children (36 months to elementary school) | 13,000 won | 23,000 won |
| Under 36 months | Free | Free |

Prices as of November 2026 from the official site. There are also early-bird (before 8 a.m.) and evening ticket types; check the official site for their current prices. Groups of 20 or more get 10% off. Some booking platforms sell the same ticket slightly cheaper in advance, but buying at the wharf is always possible.

Motor boats leave whenever at least two people are waiting, carry up to five, and run in the warmer months only. In 2026 the Gapyeong Wharf motor boat runs until November 29.

## Ferry hours

From the official ferry schedule:

| Time | Frequency |
|---|---|
| 8:00 to 9:00 | Every 30 minutes |
| 9:00 to 18:00 | Every 10 to 20 minutes |
| 18:00 to 21:00 | Every 30 minutes |

- **Base hours:** first boat to the island 8:00, last boat 21:00. First boat back 8:05, last boat back 21:05.
- **Autumn 2026 extension (September 18 to November 22):** first boat 7:30 every day, and the last boat runs until 21:30 on Fridays, Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays.
- The crossing itself takes about 5 minutes.

The island is open all year. If you come by train, the last train to Seoul matters more than the last ferry.

## The ZipWire option

Instead of the ferry, you can fly onto the island on a **ZipWire** from an 80 m tower next to the wharf.

| Course | Price | Where you land |
|---|---|---|
| Family Course | 55,000 won | Directly on Nami Island |
| Adventure Course | 58,000 won | Jaraseom, then a motor boat to Nami Island |

The ZipWire runs from about 9:00 to 18:00 and is one-way only: the fee includes a ferry ticket back from the island. Lines get long on autumn weekends, so go early. It is run by a separate operator (031-582-8091); ask them whether island admission is included before you pay.

## What to see on the island

The island is about 2 km end to end, and the main sights are close together.

- **Metasequoia Lane:** the island's best-known avenue of tall trees, golden-brown in late autumn.
- **Ginkgo and pine lanes:** bright yellow in late October and early November.
- **The riverside path:** a loop around the island with views of the Bukhan River and surrounding mountains.
- **Cafes and restaurants:** there are places to eat on the island, so you do not need to bring lunch. Gapyeong and nearby Chuncheon are known for *dakgalbi*, spicy stir-fried chicken.

Some seasonal activities end in November. In 2026 the duck pedal and rowing boats run only until November 15 plus the weekend of November 21-22, and the island's small water park is closed for the season.

## When to go

- **Autumn (late October to mid-November):** the most popular time for the foliage. It is also the most crowded, especially on weekends.
- **Winter (December to February):** quieter, and pretty after snow. Gapyeong is colder than Seoul, so dress warmly; see [Korea winter weather and what to wear](../korea-winter-weather-what-to-wear/).
- **Spring (April to May):** fresh green trees and flowers, busy on weekends.
- **Summer:** green and shady, but humid, with a rainy spell around July.

Weekday mornings are the least crowded at any time of year. Arriving before 10 a.m. means a short ferry queue.

## A simple one-day plan

1. **8:30 to 9:00:** ITX-Cheongchun from Yongsan or Cheongnyangni to Gapyeong.
2. **About 10:00:** Taxi to the wharf, buy tickets, ferry over.
3. **10:15 to 13:30:** Walk the tree lanes and riverside path, then lunch on the island.
4. **14:00:** Ferry back, taxi to Gapyeong Station.
5. **Afternoon:** Train back to Seoul, or continue to **Gangchon** for the rail bike or to **Chuncheon** for dakgalbi before heading back.

Other Gapyeong sights such as Petite France and the Garden of Morning Calm are not on the train line; you need a taxi or the Gapyeong City Tour bus to reach them, so they are easier to add with a tour.

## Tour or do it yourself?

| | Do it yourself | Organized tour |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | Train plus admission, usually cheaper | More, but includes transport |
| Flexibility | Choose your times | Fixed schedule |
| Effort | Book train, taxi to the wharf | Pickup in Seoul |
| Best for | One sight, at your own pace | Combining Nami with Petite France, the Garden of Morning Calm or the rail bike in one day |

If you only want Nami Island, going on your own by ITX-Cheongchun is straightforward. If you want two or three Gapyeong sights in one day, a tour saves a lot of taxi time.

## FAQ

**How long does it take to get from Seoul to Nami Island?**
About 1 to 1.5 hours from central Seoul: around 45 to 60 minutes on ITX-Cheongchun to Gapyeong, 5 minutes by taxi to the wharf, and a 5-minute ferry.

**How much is the Nami Island ticket?**
As of November 2026, 19,000 won for adults, including the round-trip ferry. Children from 36 months to elementary school age pay 13,000 won.

**Do I need to book Nami Island tickets in advance?**
No. You can buy them at Gapyeong Wharf. Book the ITX-Cheongchun train in advance on weekends, though.

**Can I use T-money to get to Nami Island?**
Yes on the Gyeongchun Line subway, the local bus and most taxis. ITX-Cheongchun needs a train ticket booked through Korail.

**What time is the last ferry from Nami Island?**
Normally 21:05. During the autumn 2026 extension (to November 22), boats run until 21:30 on Fridays, weekends and public holidays.

**How much time do I need on the island?**
Two to four hours is enough for the main lanes, a loop of the riverside path and lunch.

**Is Nami Island worth it in winter?**
Yes if you like quiet walks; it is much less crowded than in autumn. Without snow the trees are bare, so set your expectations accordingly.
