Olive Young is Korea's biggest health and beauty chain, and for most visitors it is the easiest place to buy Korean skincare, sunscreen, sheet masks and makeup at local prices. There are branches near almost every subway station in central Seoul, but a few tourist-focused stores make the trip noticeably smoother.

> **Quick answer:** Bring your **physical passport**, keep each receipt at **₩15,000 or more**, and ask for **"tax free"** before paying. Large tourist branches such as **Olive Young Myeongdong Town** take the VAT off at the till. Time your trip for the **Olive Young Sale** (held every **March, June, September and December**) if you can, and look for **1+1** and **2+1** tags year-round. For the widest range, go to **Olive Young N Seongsu** or **Central Myeongdong Town**. Details as of November 2026.

## Which Olive Young store should tourists visit?

Any branch sells the core products, but stock is smaller in neighborhood stores. Olive Young runs well over a hundred branches in tourist districts, and Myeongdong alone had nine as of May 2026. These are the ones worth a special trip:

| Store | Where | Hours | Why go |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Myeongdong Town** | 53 Myeongdong-gil, Jung-gu (Myeongdong) | 10:00–22:30 daily | Built for overseas visitors: English, Chinese and Japanese service, bilingual labels, immediate tax refund |
| **Central Myeongdong Town** | Myeongdong, about 2 minutes' walk from the Myeongdong Station branch | Check the app | Opened March 2026; three floors, around 15,000 products, 22 checkout counters and an 800-item sheet mask wall |
| **Olive Young N Seongsu** | 13 Yeonmujang 7-gil, Seongdong-gu (near Seongsu Station, Line 2) | 10:00–22:00 | The largest store in Korea, with skin-analysis, makeup and men's brow services |

*Addresses and hours from the Korea Tourism Organization listings, as of November 2026. Hours can change on holidays.*

A few practical notes:

- **Myeongdong Town** has been Olive Young's top-selling store, with around 95% of sales to foreign customers in 2025. That also means it is busy in the evening. Weekday mornings are the calm window.
- **Central Myeongdong Town** opened on March 26, 2026 and carries the biggest product range of any Olive Young. Staff who speak English, Chinese or Japanese wear a flag badge, so you can spot who to ask.
- **Olive Young N Seongsu** is a destination in itself. The services (skin fit, makeup and brow sessions) need a **same-day reservation**, and on-site booking opens at **11:00**. Seongsu is also one of Seoul's best areas for cafés and pop-up shops, so plan half a day.
- In **Busan**, the four-floor Nampo Town store opened in 2026 with beauty consultants on site, convenient if you are visiting Nampo-dong or Jagalchi Market.

Branches in Hongdae, Gangnam and near major hotels are also well stocked. Use the store finder in the Olive Young app or on a map app (see our [Naver Map and Kakao Map guide](../google-maps-korea-naver-kakao-map/)) to check the nearest one.

## How to get the tax refund at Olive Young

Olive Young uses Korea's standard tourist VAT refund. If you are a foreign visitor staying less than six months:

1. **Bring the real passport.** A photo or photocopy is not accepted.
2. **Spend ₩15,000 or more on one receipt.** Baskets from separate payments can't be added together.
3. **Say "tax free" before the cashier scans your items** and hand over the passport.
4. **At immediate-refund stores**, the refund is deducted on the spot and you pay the lower price. Each purchase must be under ₩1,000,000, with a ₩5,000,000 total for immediate refunds per trip.
5. **At other branches**, you get a refund receipt to claim at a downtown refund booth or the airport kiosk before you fly.

Tourist branches in Myeongdong, Hongdae and Gangnam generally offer the immediate refund. Smaller neighborhood stores may only issue a refund receipt, or none, so if tax-free shopping matters, do your big haul at a tourist branch. You must take the goods out of Korea within three months. ## When are the Olive Young sales?

| Promotion | When | What to expect |
|---|---|---|
| **Olive Young Sale** | Quarterly: March, June, September and December (usually early in the month) | Store-wide discounts across more than 1,500 brands, the biggest savings of the year |
| **1+1 and 2+1 deals** | Year-round, changing items | Buy one get one free, or three for the price of two, marked on the shelf tags |
| **Monthly featured items** | Every month | Discounted "picks of the month" on endcaps and in the app |

The next quarterly sale falls in **early December 2026**. Exact dates are announced in the Olive Young app and on in-store posters a week or two before. During sales, stores in Myeongdong and Seongsu get very crowded, so go early in the day.

Prices are already low compared with buying Korean brands abroad. Even outside a sale, the 1+1 deals on sunscreen, toner pads and sheet masks are often the best value.

## How to pay at Olive Young

Overseas Visa and Mastercard credit cards are widely accepted at Olive Young tills, and tourist branches have multilingual payment signs. Cash in won works everywhere. If your card is declined (it happens with some foreign debit cards), a prepaid travel card or cash is the simplest backup. See our [guide to paying in Korea](../paying-in-korea-cards-cash-wowpass/) for options like WOWPASS and how to avoid foreign transaction fees.

You can pay part of a large basket with one card and the rest another way, but remember the tax-free minimum applies per receipt.

## What do tourists buy at Olive Young?

Olive Young does not publish a tourist-only bestseller list, but its own store data and its yearly **Olive Young Awards** (ranked on sales) point to the same categories:

- **Skincare** is the biggest draw for international shoppers: toners, toner pads, serums and barrier creams from Korean brands.
- **Sunscreen**: lightweight Korean sunscreens are one of the most common souvenirs.
- **Sheet masks**: cheap, light and easy to pack, often sold in 1+1 bundles.
- **Color makeup**: lip tints, cushions and eyeliners from Korean brands.
- **Men's grooming and brow products**, which are especially popular at N Seongsu.

Olive Young also sells snacks, health supplements and red ginseng products, which can qualify for the tax refund too.

## Tips for a smoother Olive Young trip

- **Download the Olive Young app** to scan barcodes, read reviews and check whether an item is in stock at a specific branch.
- **Check the shelf tags carefully.** A 1+1 tag applies to the specific product named; the free item must usually be the same product.
- **Use the testers.** Many products have testers on the shelf, so try textures and shades before buying.
- **Pack liquids carefully.** Cosmetics over 100 ml can't go in carry-on luggage on international flights unless they are bought in airport duty-free after security. Put toners and larger bottles in checked baggage.
- **Shop on your last full day, not at the airport.** You'll find a far wider range in the city, and the immediate refund saves a stop at the airport kiosk.
- **Ordering from home later:** Olive Young's Global Mall (global.oliveyoung.com) ships to many countries, useful for restocking favorites after your trip.

## FAQ

**Is Olive Young cheaper than duty-free?**
Often, for Korean brands. Duty-free shops focus on luxury and international brands, while Olive Young carries mid-priced Korean skincare at local prices, plus regular 1+1 deals. Compare a few items in the app before you decide.

**Do all Olive Young stores have tax free?**
No. Tourist branches generally offer immediate refunds, but smaller branches may not. Ask at the till before paying and show your passport.

**What time does Olive Young open?**
Most central Seoul branches open around 10:00 and close between 22:00 and 22:30. Myeongdong Town is open 10:00–22:30 every day as of November 2026. Neighborhood branches can close earlier.

**Do I need to speak Korean?**
No. The big tourist branches have English, Chinese and Japanese speaking staff and bilingual signs. Elsewhere, showing a product photo on your phone works well.

**Is there a foreigner discount?**
There is no general tourist discount at the till as of November 2026. The real savings come from the quarterly sale, 1+1 and 2+1 deals and the tax refund. Myeongdong Town's service lounge has offered welcome gifts and Global Mall vouchers to visitors who sign up for an online account, so ask staff what's running.
