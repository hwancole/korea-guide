The Hwacheon Sancheoneo Ice Festival (화천 산천어축제) is Korea's best-known winter festival. Every January, the small town of Hwacheon in Gangwon State freezes over its river and lets visitors fish for *sancheoneo* (cherry salmon, a kind of mountain trout) through holes in the ice. Then you can have your catch grilled on the spot.

> **Quick answer:** The 2027 festival runs **January 9 to 31, 2027** on the frozen Hwacheoncheon Stream in Hwacheon-eup, about 90 km northeast of Seoul. Ice fishing in 2026 cost **15,000 won** (10,000 won for foreign passport holders), with about **5,000 won returned** as local vouchers. 2027 prices are not announced yet, as of November 2026. From Seoul, take an intercity bus from **Dong Seoul Terminal** to Hwacheon (about 3 hours 20 minutes on the current timetable, 18,900 won), or book a **foreigner shuttle package** that leaves from Hongdae and Myeongdong.

Dates are from the Korea Tourism Organization's November 2026 winter festival list. Prices and hours below are from the 2026 edition unless stated. Treat them as a guide, and check the official site (narafestival.com) once the 2027 details come out.

## How ice fishing works

Ice fishing is the main event. The river is divided into fishing zones with holes already drilled in the ice. You sit or squat by a hole, drop a small lure, and jig it up and down. Staff release fish into the river throughout the day.

1. **Get a ticket.** Book online in advance, or buy one at the venue for the on-site fishing areas. In 2026, tickets were also sold through Korean ticket sites such as NOL (Yanolja), which closed sales at 23:59 the day before.
2. **Get gear.** Bring your own simple rod or buy or rent one at the venue. Tour packages usually include a rental rod.
3. **Find your zone.** If you have a reservation, go to your assigned zone and time. If not, use the on-site zone. Foreign visitors can use the dedicated foreigner fishing and rest area.
4. **Fish.** Tour operators say the limit is three fish per person. Follow staff instructions, because the rule on taking fish away can change from year to year.
5. **Grill your catch.** Take your fish to the grilling centers near the fishing grounds. Staff clean and grill it for a fee, or salt-grill it as part of a package.

### Ticket prices (2026 edition)

| Ticket | Price |
|---|---|
| General (middle school age and up) | 15,000 won |
| Elementary school children | 10,000 won |
| Foreign passport holders, seniors 65+, and other discount groups | 10,000 won |
| Preschool children with a guardian | Free |

Part of the fee comes back as vouchers. In 2026 it was about **5,000 won**, given as Hwacheon farm-product vouchers and Hwacheon local gift certificates you can spend at local shops and stalls. Effectively, the fishing itself cost about 10,000 won for most adults.

**Change ahead for 2027:** In October 2026, the county said it was considering raising the adult fee to around **25,000 won**, while increasing the voucher refund to around **15,000 won**. That would keep the real cost similar but give you more to spend in town. This was still under review as of November 2026, so check the final prices before you book.

### Night fishing

The fishing grounds also open at night, so you can try again if the daytime fish do not bite. In 2026, guides listed night fishing on weekends from 18:00 to 21:00, with reservations. Check the 2027 schedule when it is published.

## Other things to do at the festival

You do not have to fish to enjoy the day. The 2026 festival also had:

- **Bare-hand catching.** Participants wade into a cold pool in shorts and T-shirts and catch fish with their hands. It is the festival's signature spectacle, and fun to watch even if you do not join.
- **Sledding.** Tube sleds on a 40 m snow slope and a 60 m ice track on the frozen river, plus traditional ice sleds.
- **Ice bobsleigh, ice soccer, curling and a zipline** across the festival grounds.
- **Ice lantern plaza.** Large ice and snow sculptures made with artisans from Harbin, China, inside the Seohwasan multipurpose indoor plaza. It is a good place to warm up.
- **Lantern street (Seondeung Street).** Thousands of fish-shaped lanterns over the main street of Hwacheon-eup, lit up at night, with a night festival on weekends.
- **Santa Claus post office.** A replica of the one in Rovaniemi, Finland.

Some extras such as sledding cost a few thousand won each. For 2027, the county has said it is looking at new night programs, such as drone and laser shows, but these were not confirmed as of November 2026.

| Shuttle bus package | Hongik Univ. Station, Myeongdong Station | About 2.5-3 h each way | Package price | Easiest; includes fishing ticket |
| Car | Seoul | About 2 h without traffic | Fuel, tolls | Expect heavy weekend traffic and icy roads |

Bus times and the fare are from a timetable checked in November 2026 (weekday, one adult). Buses back to Dong Seoul run 8 times a day from about 07:40 to 19:30. Festival seasons often bring extra services and full buses, so buy your ticket in advance and confirm times with the terminal. The festival is in Hwacheon-eup, the same town as the bus terminal. Ask at the terminal for directions, or take a short taxi ride if you are unsure.

### The foreigner shuttle package

If you want the simplest option, look at the shuttle bus packages sold to foreign visitors by agencies such as Trazy. The 2027 version runs every day from January 9 to 31:

- **Pickup:** Hongik Univ. Station Exit 4 at 08:30, then Myeongdong Station Exit 2 at 09:00.
- **At the festival:** about 11:30 to 17:00.
- **Back in Seoul:** about 19:00 at Myeongdong and 19:30 at Hongdae, depending on traffic.
- **Included:** round-trip bus, an English or Chinese-speaking staff member, the ice fishing ticket, rod rental, one grilled fish, snow and ice sled rides, entry to the ice sculpture plaza, and a 5,000 won festival voucher.
- **Rules:** foreign passport holders only. It needs at least 20 people to run. Full refunds are available up to 3 days before.

This is not a recommendation of one company. Several Seoul tour operators sell similar day tours, so compare what is included.

## What to wear

Hwacheon is one of the coldest towns in South Korea, and you will be sitting still on ice for an hour or more. January mornings can drop below -10°C.

- A long, warm down coat, thermal base layers and warm trousers.
- Waterproof, insulated boots with good grip. The ice is slippery and can be wet on top.
- Gloves you can fish in, plus a warmer pair. Fingerless gloves with mitten covers work well.
- A hat, a neck warmer, and hand warmers (sold in every Korean convenience store).
- A small folding stool or mat, as the ice is cold to sit on.

## Tips for a smooth visit

- **Go on a weekday.** In 2025, the festival had about 1.86 million visitors, including about 122,000 from abroad. Weekends are much busier.
- **Arrive early.** The on-site zones fill up as the day goes on, especially on weekends.
- **Bring cash and a card.** Cards work at most stalls, but cash helps at smaller ones. See our [guide to paying in Korea](../paying-in-korea-cards-cash-wowpass/).
- **Spend your vouchers in town.** They are for Hwacheon shops and stalls, not for use back in Seoul.
- **Check the ice news.** The county monitors ice thickness daily. If the ice is too thin, the festival can delay opening, switch to open-water fishing, or reduce activities.
- **Combine with skiing?** Hwacheon is not near the main ski resorts. If you want both, plan separate days. See [skiing in Korea from Seoul](../skiing-in-korea-from-seoul/).

## FAQ

**When is the Hwacheon Sancheoneo Ice Festival in 2027?**
January 9 to 31, 2027, according to the Korea Tourism Organization and Hwacheon County.

**How much does ice fishing cost?**
In 2026, 15,000 won for adults and 10,000 won for foreign passport holders, with about 5,000 won returned as local vouchers. The 2027 fee may change. In October 2026 the county was considering about 25,000 won with a larger refund of about 15,000 won.

**Do I need to book in advance?**
Not necessarily. There are reservation zones and on-site zones. Booking in advance is safer for weekends. Tour packages handle the ticket for you.

**Is there an area for foreigners?**
Yes. In 2026 the festival ran foreigner-only fishing and rest areas.

**Can I eat the fish I catch?**
Yes. Grilling centers at the venue will clean and grill your fish, usually for a small fee. Packages often include one grilled fish.

**How do I get there by public transport?**
Take an intercity bus from Dong Seoul Terminal to Hwacheon. On the November 2026 timetable it takes about 3 hours 20 minutes and costs 18,900 won. Alternatively, take the ITX-Cheongchun train to Chuncheon and continue by bus or taxi.

**Can I do it as a day trip from Seoul?**
Yes. Most foreign visitors go on a day tour or shuttle package, which leaves Seoul around 08:30 and returns around 19:00 to 19:30.

**What happens if the ice is too thin?**
The festival can delay opening, move fishing to open water, or reduce activities. Check the official site or social media before you leave.

**Who can I call for help in English?**
The Korea Travel Helpline, 1330 (+82-2-1330 from a foreign phone), answers in English and other languages.
