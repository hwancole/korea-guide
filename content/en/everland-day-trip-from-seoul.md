Everland (에버랜드) is Korea's biggest theme park, in Yongin, about 40 km south of Seoul. It is known for the wooden roller coaster T Express, the Safari World and Lost Valley animal rides, seasonal festivals and Panda World. You can do it as an easy day trip on public transport without joining a tour.

> **Quick answer:** From Gangnam, take the Gyeonggi express **bus 5002** from Gangnam or Sinnonhyeon Station. It goes straight to the Everland bus terminal near the main gate in roughly 40 to 70 minutes, depending on traffic. From Seoul Station, Myeongdong or Itaewon, the newer **bus 4105** (launched August 2026) takes about an hour. Pay with T-money or WOWPASS as you board. As of November 2026, a full-day ticket at the official list price is **65,000 won for adults in November** and **55,000 won in December**. In November and early December the park opens at **10:00** and closes at **20:00 Sunday to Thursday** and **21:00 on Fridays and Saturdays**.

Prices and hours below are as of November 2026. Everland changes its prices by month and its hours by season, so check the official app or site before you go.

### Option 1: Bus 5002 from Gangnam (the classic route)

Bus 5002 is a red Gyeonggi express bus that runs between Gangnam and Yongin and stops at Everland.

1. **Find the stop.** In Seoul, the bus starts near Sinnonhyeon Station and stops at Gangnam Station on Gangnam-daero, in the central bus lane. Search for "5002" in Naver Map or Kakao Map to see the live stop and arrival time (see our [map apps guide](../google-maps-korea-naver-kakao-map/)).
2. **Tap your card when you board**, and again when you get off so any distance charge is calculated correctly. The fare is a regular Gyeonggi express-bus fare of a few thousand won.
3. **Get off at the Everland bus terminal** (에버랜드 노선버스터미널). It was moved closer to the main gate in 2020, so it is a short walk to the entrance.

One quirk to know: since mid-2024 the route is split into two variants by time of day. Trips from Seoul run all day, but the morning variant (5002A) heading back to Gangnam does not stop at Everland, and only the afternoon and evening variant (5002B) does. In practice this means the bus home works from the afternoon onward, which is when you will want it anyway. The last buses back toward Seoul leave late in the evening. Check the exact last bus in your map app before the park closes.

These are seated express buses, so when one is full you wait for the next, and queues at Gangnam on weekend mornings and at the Everland terminal at closing time can be long. Arriving at the stop before about 9:00 helps.

### Option 2: Bus 4105 from Seoul Station and Myeongdong (new in 2026)

In August 2026 a new direct express bus, **4105**, started running from the **Seoul Station Bus Transfer Center**, via **Myeongdong**, **Sungnyemun**, **Namdaemun Market** and **Itaewon**, then down the expressway to Everland. The operator expected the trip to take about an hour, compared with 1 hour 40 minutes or more with transfers before.

This is the easiest option if you are staying north of the Han River. Search "4105" in Naver Map or Kakao Map for the nearest stop and timetable, and tap your card as with any other bus.

### Option 3: Subway and the EverLine (the traffic-proof option)

1. Take the subway to **Giheung Station** (기흥) on the Suin-Bundang Line. From Gangnam, the fastest way is usually the Shinbundang Line to Jeongja, then the Suin-Bundang Line to Giheung.
2. Transfer to the **EverLine** (용인경전철), a small automated light rail, and ride to the last stop, **Jeondae-Everland** (전대·에버랜드), about 30 minutes.
3. From the station, Everland runs a **free shuttle bus** toward the park. Follow the Everland signs from the station.

You pay the whole trip with the same transport card (see our [Seoul subway guide](../seoul-subway-guide/) and [payments guide](../paying-in-korea-cards-cash-wowpass/)). It takes longer than the bus, but trains do not get stuck in weekend traffic on the Gyeongbu Expressway.

### Option 4: Shuttle packages and taxis

Travel platforms such as Klook sell round-trip shuttles from central Seoul, often bundled with a park ticket. They are convenient if you do not want to handle buses, but you are tied to a fixed return time. A taxi from Gangnam is possible but expensive, and getting one back from Everland at closing time can be slow. See our [taxi guide](../taxi-in-korea-kakao-t-guide/) for the Kakao T app.

## Everland ticket prices (November 2026 to December 2026)

Everland now sets its list price **by month**. The older A, B, C and D date-tier price chart you may still see online is out of date.

| Ticket (official list price) | November 2026 | December 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Full-day, adult (13+) and teen | 65,000 won | 55,000 won |
| Full-day, child (36 months to 12) and senior (65+) | 55,000 won | 45,000 won |
| Under 36 months | Free (bring a passport for proof of age) | Free |

Two other ticket types can be booked through Everland's own reservation system:

- **Afternoon ticket (오후권):** entry from **15:00**, from **34,000 won** for adults in November 2026.
- **Night ticket (야간권):** entry from **17:00**, listed at **28,000 won** in late October 2026.

With the park closing at 20:00 on many winter days, an evening ticket gives you only a few hours, and late entrants may find the popular animal rides already full. For a first visit, a full-day ticket is worth it.

**Buying online is usually cheaper.** Klook and other travel platforms regularly sell foreign-visitor day passes well below the gate price, with a QR code you scan at the entrance. Prices change often, so compare a few on the day you book. Korean card-company discounts mostly need a Korean-issued card, so they rarely help visitors.

Everland publishes hours a few weeks at a time and extends them around Christmas and public holidays, so check the calendar in the app for your date. Some rides close for maintenance on set days, and several outdoor kids' areas close for the winter from December 1. If there is one ride you must do, check its status in the app before you travel.

## Tips for a smoother day

- **Install the Everland app.** It shows live wait times, the show schedule, ride closures and the park map in English.
- **Use Smart Queue (스마트줄서기).** It is free: after you enter, register your ticket in the app and reserve a time slot for popular rides instead of standing in line. Slots go quickly, so do it as soon as you are through the gate.
- **Paid priority passes.** Everland also sells paid "Q-Pass" priority boarding in some packages and separate Plan-it priority bundles. They are only worth it on crowded weekends.
- **Go to Safari World and Lost Valley early.** These animal rides are among the most popular and queues grow fast after late morning.
- **Panda World:** since October 2026, viewing is in timed sessions of about 10 minutes, with a bag check at the entrance. Expect a queue on weekends.
- **Dress for the cold.** Yongin is in the hills and evenings in December can drop well below freezing. See our [winter weather guide](../korea-winter-weather-what-to-wear/).
- **Go on a weekday.** Weekends and Korean school holidays are much busier, and Christmas Eve and Christmas Day are among the busiest days of the year.
- **Caribbean Bay**, the water park next door, needs a separate ticket.

## FAQ

**How long does it take to get from Seoul to Everland?**
About 40 to 70 minutes by bus 5002 from Gangnam, depending on traffic, and about an hour by bus 4105 from Seoul Station and Myeongdong. By subway and EverLine, allow 1.5 to 2 hours from central Seoul.

**How much is an Everland ticket?**
As of November 2026, the official full-day price is 65,000 won for adults in November and 55,000 won in December. Children and seniors pay 10,000 won less. Online travel platforms often sell cheaper foreign-visitor tickets.

**Can I use T-money to get to Everland?**
Yes. The 5002, 4105 and 5700 buses, the subway and the EverLine all take T-money and WOWPASS.

**What time does Everland close in winter?**
From November 9 to at least December 13, 2026, it closes at 20:00 from Sunday to Thursday and 21:00 on Fridays and Saturdays. Hours are longer around Christmas and holidays.

**Is Everland open in winter?**
Yes, all year. Some outdoor rides close in very cold weather or for maintenance, and some kids' areas close for the season, but the main rides, animal attractions and winter lights run through the winter.

**Is Everland better than Lotte World?**
They suit different trips. Lotte World is mostly indoors in central Seoul, which is handy in cold or rainy weather. Everland is a full-day outdoor trip with bigger coasters and the safari and panda attractions.
